Scan, or open radhepa.github.io/jutsu-simulator. Prop the phone up so both hands fit in frame.

Sign guide

The model knows these fourteen seals. Hold each one still for a beat; it counts after about a quarter second. To repeat a seal, drop your hands briefly first.

Camera tips

  • Light your hands from the front. A window behind you turns them into silhouettes.
  • Hands at chest height, about an arm's length from the lens, fully inside the frame.
  • Plain background helps. Busy patterns behind your hands lower the confidence.
  • Watch the readout. If a seal sits under 60%, rotate your wrists slightly toward the camera.
  • Freestyle resolves on pause. Stop signing for about a second and the sequence is judged.
